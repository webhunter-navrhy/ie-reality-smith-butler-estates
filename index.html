About the property

Description property

Smith & Butler Estates are delighted to present to the market this beautifully presented and renovated three-bedroom end-of-terrace home, presented in excellent walk-in condition throughout.

Finished to an impressive standard from top to bottom, the property has been thoughtfully extended and reconfigured to create a spacious and practical home. A two-storey extension to the rear provides a generously sized kitchen at ground-floor level and a spacious double bedroom upstairs, while the redesigned ground floor also incorporates a separate laundry area and convenient guest W.C.

The ground floor comprises a welcoming open-plan living and dining area, with a feature fireplace creating an attractive focal point. The dining area benefits from built-in storage, while the stylishly finished kitchen features granite worktops, a freestanding five-burner gas cooker and ample wall and floor cabinetry, with direct access to the rear garden and side laneway. A separate laundry area and convenient guest W.C. complete the ground-floor accommodation. Upstairs, there are three well-proportioned bedrooms. The bright, south-facing primary bedroom benefits from fitted wardrobes and its own en-suite, while the generous double bedroom within the rear extension also features fitted wardrobes. A further single bedroom and main family bathroom complete the first-floor accommodation.

The property also benefits from a floored attic, accessed via a staircase, providing valuable additional space.

Ideally located, the property enjoys a wealth of amenities within easy reach, including schools, shops, churches, pubs and restaurants. Excellent bus routes provide convenient access to Dublin City Centre, approximately four miles away, while a variety of sporting and recreational facilities are also nearby. The location further benefits from easy access to the M1, M50 and Dublin Airport, with DCU just a five-minute walk away. Offering excellent presentation, generous accommodation and a highly convenient location, this is a superb opportunity to acquire a turnkey home ready for immediate occupation.

Viewing is strongly advised to fully appreciate all that this exceptional property has to offer.
